US Trade Intelligence

What US Importers Need to Know Specifically

Beyond the generic sourcing process, successful India-to-US pouffes programmes hinge on four country-specific realities: the India–US shipping corridor, the duty and trade-agreement landscape, US-specific textile and product compliance, and US retailer commit cycles. Each is covered below.

Trade Lane

India-to-US Ocean Routing

Indian cushions reach the US on two principal routings. Via Suez to East/Gulf Coast: New York–Newark (28–32 days), Savannah (30–34 days) and Houston (32–36 days) on Maersk, MSC, Hapag-Lloyd and ONE services. Via Pacific / Trans-Pacific: Los Angeles / Long Beach (28–32 days via Singapore transhipment), Oakland and Seattle for inland-empire and Pacific-NW distribution. Red Sea diversions have added 10–14 days to Suez routings in recent periods; Pacific routings have become cost-competitive as a result.

Duty & FTA

US Import Duty & Origin

Pouffes and floor seating classify under HS 9401.80 (other seats) for structured floor seating and HS 6304.92/6304.93 for cushion-construction pouffes. The US does not currently have a free-trade agreement with India. India's GSP (Generalized System of Preferences) eligibility was terminated by the US in 2019, so MFN duties apply to Indian-origin home textiles — typically in the mid-single-digit percentage range for HS Chapter 63 lines, depending on composition. The US Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTSUS) classification should be verified per shipment; we provide the HS breakdown with every commercial invoice. Importers of record should ensure compliance with Section 301 tariffs are not applicable (Chapter 63 home textiles of Indian origin are not subject to Section 301, which targets China-origin goods).

Compliance

US-Specific Regulation

Five US regimes are relevant. The Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) enforces 16 CFR 1632 (mattress flammability) and 16 CFR 1633 (mattress pad and bedding standards). The FTC Textile Fiber Products Identification Act mandates fibre-composition, country-of-origin and manufacturer identification labels. California Proposition 65 imposes additional warning obligations for chemical content. TSCA (Toxic Substances Control Act) covers chemical restrictions. Major US retailers additionally require CPSIA compliance for cushions marketed to children, and social audits through Sedex SMETA or WRAP. We supply the relevant test reports and certificates per shipment.

Commit Cycle

US Buying Calendar

US home-textile buyers commit Spring/Summer ranges in August–September for February delivery and Fall/Holiday ranges in January–February for July–August delivery. Evergreen core programmes run continuous replenishment. The primary US sourcing moments are NY NOW (New York Market Center) in February and August, High Point Market (North Carolina, April and October) for furniture-adjacent home textiles, and Las Vegas Market (January and July) for West-Coast-based buyers. Ambiente Frankfurt (February) and Heimtextil (January) draw a significant US-buyer contingent for European sourcing. The LA Mart hosts West-Coast-focused trade events year-round.

US Buyer FAQ

Questions US Importers Ask

Which US ports does Abhi Home ship pouffes and floor seating to?

We ship from Mundra and Nhava Sheva in India to Los Angeles / Long Beach, New York / Newark, Savannah in United States. Typical ocean transit is 28 to 35 days plus customs clearance. For smaller orders we can consolidate via LCL groupage services direct to US inland container depots or bonded warehouses.

What is the MOQ for private-label pouffes and floor seating to US?

Minimum order quantity depends on construction. We support low MOQs to accommodate hand-finished and machine-made pouffe constructions, sized to your programme. Sampling-stage MOQs are kept low to support buyer development cycles. For first-container pilot runs we accept blended MOQs across shapes and fabric constructions.

Are your pouffes and floor seating compliant with US retailer requirements?

Yes. We manufacture to OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GOTS, GRS and BCI certifications, and supply CPSIA, California Prop 65, FTC fibre-content labelling and Country-of-Origin marking documentation per shipment. For flammability-sensitive categories (including 16 CFR 1610 where applicable) we supply third-party test reports from accredited US-recognised labs.

What is the lead time from order confirmation to US delivery?

Typical lead time is 85 to 110 days from order confirmation to US port: 45 to 65 days for production including sampling approval, plus 28 to 35 days of ocean freight. Expedited timelines are possible for repeat programmes where fabric, print separations and construction are already approved.

Can you handle US retailer labelling, tickets and packaging?

Yes. Our in-house finishing team produces swing tags, care labels, barcoded SKU tickets and retailer-specific inner and outer packaging. We regularly ship floor-ready, shelf-ready and dropship-ready pouffes and floor seating for US retailers with bespoke compliance artwork in the required language(s).
