Heritage & Scale

Six Decades of Bath Mat & Shower Curtain Manufacturing from India

Abhi Home has manufactured home textiles in India since 1965. Across three generations, the business has grown from a single-loom operation into a vertically integrated private-label OEM — running fabric development, printing, dyeing, embroidery, making-up and QA under one roof. We supply bath mats and shower curtains to international retailers, importers and hospitality groups across Europe, North America, Asia-Pacific and the Middle East.

The business operates entirely through private-label contract manufacturing. We do not sell under our own brand in retail. Every programme runs under the buyer's care label, tech pack, packaging spec and compliance framework — with full chain-of-custody documentation against OEKO-TEX, GOTS and GRS where required.

Commercial Terms

MOQ, Lead Time & Sampling

Minimum Order

Low MOQs across our bath mat and shower curtain ranges to suit programme sizes from boutique retail through to hospitality contracts. Sampling-stage MOQs are kept low to support buyer development cycles. Mixed-design container programmes are supported for established buyers.

Lead Time

60–75 days post sample approval for bulk. Ocean transit adds 21–35 days to most buyer markets; air-freight is available for urgent programmes at a cost premium.

Sampling

Counter-samples in 10–14 days; pre-production samples in 21 days. Pre-production samples are sent to the buyer's DC or nominated test lab before bulk release.

Incoterms

FOB Mundra or Nhava Sheva as standard. CIF, CFR, DDP and DDP-DC available on request. US buyers can additionally contract through our US trading entity for domestic-invoicing programmes.

Why India

Why International Buyers Source Bath Mats & Shower Curtains From India

India is one of the world's largest producers of cotton bath textiles. The country's long-staple cotton supply (particularly from Gujarat and Maharashtra), deep tufting and weaving capacity, advanced printing infrastructure and well-established OEKO-TEX and GOTS chain-of-custody systems make it the dominant sourcing origin for bath mats and shower curtains for international retailers.

Beyond the raw supply-chain advantages, India offers something structurally unique: a simultaneous mastery of hand-craft technique and industrial-scale compliance. A certified Indian manufacturer can produce 500 pieces of hand-block-printed cotton on Monday and 5,000 pieces of power-loom jacquard on Tuesday — under the same OEKO-TEX scope, the same SMETA audit, and the same vendor-portal compliance framework. No other sourcing region offers this combination.

Frequently Asked

Buyer Questions

What bath mat constructions do you produce in India?

Tufted (machine and hand-tufted), cut & loop pile, plush pile, ribbed and patterned pile (cobblestone, chevron, brick-grid, damask, bubble-circle), embroidered, scalloped edge and ruffled edge bath mats. Non-slip latex backing is standard. Bases include 100% cotton, cotton-bamboo and cotton-viscose blends; GOTS organic and GRS recycled on request.

What sizes do Indian bath mat manufacturers produce?

Standard sizes include 50×80 cm and 60×90 cm, alongside many country-specific dimensions used by retailers in the UK, US, Germany, France and other markets. Abhi Home is flexible on custom sizes — runner-length, bath-set (mat plus contour), and oversized configurations are all available.

What shower curtain types do Indian manufacturers supply?

Decorative cotton shower curtains (printed, woven, waffle-weave, embroidered, jacquard) and 100% polyester shower curtains (printed and plain). Standard sizes 180×180 cm and 180×200 cm; custom dimensions available. Eyelet, hook-tab or tie-top headers. Custom prints produced from buyer artwork.

Are bath mats and shower curtains from India OEKO-TEX certified?

Yes. All bath mat and shower curtain production is run under OEKO-TEX Standard 100 scope. Abhi Home additionally supports GOTS organic cotton and GRS recycled-content programmes with full chain-of-custody documentation. Sedex SMETA, SA 8000 and BSCI social-compliance audits are also held at facility scope.

How long is the lead time for bath mat and shower curtain production from India?

60–75 days for bulk production after sample approval. Counter-samples in 10–14 days; pre-production samples in 21 days. Add 21–35 days ocean transit to most buyer markets. Total order-to-DC is typically 95–120 days for first-run programmes; repeat programmes run on shortened lead times with pre-approved fabric and dye lots.
