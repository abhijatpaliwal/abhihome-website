US Trade Logistics

How Bedding Programmes Move from India to US Shelves

Origin Ports

We despatch out of Mundra and Nhava Sheva (JNPT), India's two largest container ports, with direct and transhipment sailings to US on Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, ONE and Hapag-Lloyd services.

US Entry Ports

Los Angeles / Long Beach for West Coast and inland-empire DCs, New York / Newark for East Coast consolidation, Savannah for South-East retail DCs, and Houston for Gulf and Texas distribution.

Transit & Lead Time

28–35 days ocean transit plus customs. Total order-to-DC lead time is typically 85–110 days from confirmation, depending on fabric development and sampling approval.

Compliance Docs

CPSIA (Consumer Product Safety Improvement Act), California Prop 65, FTC fibre-content labelling, Country-of-Origin marking, 16 CFR flammability reports where applicable, SEDEX SMETA audit reports and OEKO-TEX certificates of compliance issued per shipment.

Contracting & Billing

US Buyer FAQ

Questions US Importers Ask

Which US ports does Abhi Home ship bedding programmes to?

We ship from Mundra and Nhava Sheva in India to Los Angeles / Long Beach, New York / Newark, Savannah in United States. Typical ocean transit is 28 to 35 days plus customs clearance. For smaller orders we can consolidate via LCL groupage services direct to US inland container depots or bonded warehouses.

What is the MOQ for private-label bedding programmes to US?

Minimum order quantity depends on construction. Printed bedding typically starts at 300 pieces per SKU. Yarn-dyed and jacquard bedding starts at 500–1,000 pieces per SKU depending on loom width. For first-container pilot runs we accept blended MOQs across a curated range of SKUs.

Are your bedding programmes compliant with US retailer requirements?

Yes. We manufacture to OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GOTS, GRS and BCI certifications, and supply CPSIA, California Prop 65, FTC fibre-content labelling and Country-of-Origin marking documentation per shipment. For flammability-sensitive categories (including 16 CFR 1632/1633 where applicable) we supply third-party test reports from accredited US-recognised labs.

What is the lead time from order confirmation to US delivery?

Typical lead time is 85 to 110 days from order confirmation to US port: 45 to 65 days for production including sampling approval, plus 28 to 35 days of ocean freight. Expedited timelines are possible for repeat programmes where fabric, print separations and construction are already approved.

Can you handle US retailer labelling, tickets and packaging?

Yes. Our in-house finishing team produces swing tags, care labels, barcoded SKU tickets and retailer-specific inner and outer packaging. We regularly ship floor-ready, shelf-ready and dropship-ready bedding programmes for US retailers with bespoke compliance artwork in the required language(s).
