Buyer Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your kids' bath mats safe for children?

They are OEKO-TEX Standard 100 tested for harmful substances, and we build them to a stricter construction brief than our adult range: no small detachable parts, no loose trims, no beads or buttons, and no glued-on features. Appliqué and embroidery are stitched to withstand pulling. Where a specific market has an additional children's product requirement, tell us at sampling and we will build to it.

Should kids' bath mats be organic?

Commercially, it is the single biggest purchase driver in this category — parents look for certification on children's product far more than on their own. We default to recommending GOTS certified organic cotton for kids' bath mats for exactly that reason. See our organic bath mats page for what the certificate actually covers.

Can you make a bath mat in our own character or brand shape?

Yes. Send a flat vector file, or even a clean scaled sketch, and we will prototype the silhouette. We will tell you honestly if a shape is impractical to cut and bind at volume, and suggest the nearest workable version. We will not reproduce a third party's licensed character without evidence you hold the licence.

What sizes work best for children's bath mats?

40×60 cm and 45×70 cm are the practical running sizes. Shaped mats are quoted to the bounding box rather than the silhouette, so tell us the overall footprint you need on shelf and we will work back from that.

Can you coordinate kids' bath mats with towels and other items?

Yes, and it is how the category sells best. We can match hooded towels, wash mitts, shower curtains and nursery cushions to the same palette and motif family, so the range merchandises as a set rather than a single SKU.

Do you supply kids' bath mats with non-slip backing?

Yes, and we generally recommend it for this category. Standard non-slip latex, or latex-free and TPR alternatives where a retailer specification requires it. Note that standard latex backing is not GOTS-compliant — if you want both organic certification and a non-slip back, we will talk you through the options at sampling.
