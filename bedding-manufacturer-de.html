Germany Trade Intelligence

What Germany Importers Need to Know Specifically

Beyond the generic sourcing process, successful India-to-Germany bedding programmes hinge on four country-specific realities: the India–Germany shipping corridor, the duty and trade-agreement landscape, Germany-specific textile and product compliance, and Germany retailer commit cycles. Each is covered below.

Trade Lane

India-to-Germany Ocean Routing

Weekly direct sailings from Mundra (Gujarat) via the Suez Canal reach Hamburg in 22–24 days on Maersk, MSC, ONE and Hapag-Lloyd services; Bremerhaven adds a day for deep-sea consolidation. Many German catalogue and e-commerce buyers prefer Rotterdam transhipment for Rhine-corridor DC distribution — a barge-feeder service moves containers up the Rhine to Duisburg, Mannheim and Frankfurt within 2–3 days of discharge. Wilhelmshaven (JadeWeserPort) handles ultra-large vessels and is the fastest lane for Baltic onward movements.

Duty & FTA

Germany Import Duty & Origin

Cushions classify under HS 6302.21 (printed cotton bed linen), HS 6302.31 (cotton bed linen, other) and HS 6302.22/6302.32 (synthetic bed linen). Germany imports under the EU Common Customs Tariff; India does not currently enjoy FTA preferential access to the EU (the India–EU FTA has been under negotiation and is the most-watched trade file for Indian textile exporters to Germany). MFN duties on HS Chapter 63 home-textile lines apply. German import VAT (19%) is levied on landed value. EU Generalised Scheme of Preferences (GSP+) and country-of-origin rules should be verified per shipment with your customs broker; we provide the HS breakdown on every commercial invoice.

Compliance

Germany-Specific Regulation

Four EU and national instruments are relevant. EU REACH Regulation 1907/2006 restricts SVHC, AZO dyes and other listed substances — German buyers routinely require OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certification as baseline. EU Textile Labelling Regulation 1007/2011 mandates fibre-composition labels in German (and other EU languages as applicable). LFGB (Lebensmittel-, Bedarfsgegenstände- und Futtermittelgesetzbuch) sections 30 & 31 apply to textiles in contact with skin. And the German Supply Chain Due Diligence Act (LkSG), in force since 2023, obliges larger retailers to audit their tier-1 suppliers for human-rights and environmental compliance — we provide Sedex SMETA 4-pillar reports per shipment.

Commit Cycle

Germany Buying Calendar

German home-textile buyers commit AW ranges in January–February for August–September on-shelf delivery, and SS ranges in August for February delivery. Catalogue and e-commerce retailers run continuous replenishment on core programmes. The single most important sourcing moment for Germany-bound home textiles is Heimtextil Frankfurt (mid-January) — the global home-textile trade show where German buyers finalise supplier shortlists. Other relevant events: Ambiente Frankfurt (February, gift & giftware), Christmasworld (January, seasonal), and the Intertextile Shanghai Home Textiles (late August) that many German buyers attend for Asian sourcing.

Germany Buyer FAQ

Questions Germany Importers Ask

Which Germany ports does Abhi Home ship bedding programmes to?

We ship from Mundra and Nhava Sheva in India to Hamburg, Bremerhaven, Rotterdam in Germany. Typical ocean transit is 22 to 26 days plus customs clearance. For smaller orders we can consolidate via LCL groupage services direct to Germany inland container depots or bonded warehouses.

What is the MOQ for private-label bedding programmes to Germany?

Minimum order quantity depends on construction. Printed bedding typically starts at 300 pieces per SKU. Yarn-dyed and jacquard bedding starts at 500–1,000 pieces per SKU depending on loom width. For first-container pilot runs we accept blended MOQs across a curated range of SKUs.

Are your bedding programmes compliant with Germany retailer requirements?

Yes. Our manufacturing is OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GOTS, GRS and BCI certified — the core certifications specified by German department stores, home-furnishing chains and design-led brands. We also supply EU REACH-compliant documentation, LkSG (German Supply Chain Act) due-diligence evidence, and Grüner Knopf-aligned documentation on request.

What is the lead time from order confirmation to Germany delivery?

Typical lead time is 75 to 95 days from order confirmation to Germany port: 45 to 60 days for production including sampling approval, plus 22 to 26 days of ocean freight. Expedited timelines are possible for repeat programmes where fabric, print separations and construction are already approved.

Can you handle Germany retailer labelling, tickets and packaging?

Yes. Our in-house finishing team produces swing tags, care labels, barcoded SKU tickets and retailer-specific inner and outer packaging. We regularly ship floor-ready, shelf-ready and dropship-ready bedding programmes for Germany retailers with bespoke compliance artwork in the required language(s).
