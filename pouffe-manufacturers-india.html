Heritage & Scale

Six Decades of Pouffe Manufacturing from India

Abhi Home has manufactured home textiles in India since 1965. Across three generations, the business has grown from a single-loom operation into a vertically integrated private-label OEM — running fabric development, printing, dyeing, embroidery, making-up and QA under one roof. We supply pouffe to international retailers, importers and hospitality groups across Europe, North America, Asia-Pacific and the Middle East.

The business operates entirely through private-label contract manufacturing. We do not sell under our own brand in retail. Every programme runs under the buyer's care label, tech pack, packaging spec and compliance framework — with full chain-of-custody documentation against OEKO-TEX, GOTS and GRS where required.

Commercial Terms

MOQ, Lead Time & Sampling

Minimum Order

Low MOQs across cover-only and filled-and-assembled pouffes, sized to programme requirements. Mixed-design container programmes are supported for established buyers.

Lead Time

60–75 days post sample approval for bulk. Ocean transit adds 21–35 days to most buyer markets; air-freight is available for urgent programmes at a cost premium.

Sampling

Counter-samples in 14 days; pre-production samples in 21–28 days. Pre-production samples are sent to the buyer's DC or nominated test lab before bulk release.

Incoterms

FOB Mundra or Nhava Sheva as standard. CIF, CFR, DDP and DDP-DC available on request. US buyers can additionally contract through our US trading entity for domestic-invoicing programmes.

Why India

Why International Buyers Source Pouffes & Floor Seating From India

India's hand-craft heritage in weaving, knitting, crochet and patchwork makes it the dominant sourcing origin for pouffes and floor seating. Indian manufacturers uniquely combine artisanal construction with retail-scale QA and OEKO-TEX compliance.

Beyond the raw supply-chain advantages, India offers something structurally unique: a simultaneous mastery of hand-craft technique and industrial-scale compliance. A certified Indian manufacturer can produce 500 pieces of hand-block-printed cotton on Monday and 5,000 pieces of power-loom jacquard on Tuesday — under the same OEKO-TEX scope, the same SMETA audit, and the same vendor-portal compliance framework. No other sourcing region offers this combination.

Frequently Asked

Buyer Questions

What is the MOQ for Indian pouffe manufacturers?

MOQs are kept low across cover-only, filled-and-assembled, hand-knitted and crochet pouffe constructions. Sampling-stage MOQs are kept lowest to support buyer development cycles.

Can Indian manufacturers produce pouffes with EPS bead filling?

Yes. Abhi Home and other compliant Indian manufacturers offer both cover-only and filled assemblies with EPS bead, shredded foam or recycled PET fill. Filling is shipped under the same compliance framework as the cover.

What fabrics are Indian pouffes typically made from?

Cotton is the dominant fabric. Jute, wool blends, recycled cotton (GRS) and cotton canvas are all standard. Cover fabrics can match the fibre composition of existing cushion programmes for visual coherence across a buyer's range.

Do Indian pouffe manufacturers offer custom embroidery and patchwork?

Yes. Patchwork, kantha embroidery, hand-tufting and block-printed decorative pouffes are staple categories. Custom embroidered logos and branded care labels are supported.

How long is lead time for pouffe production from India?

14 days for counter-samples, 21–28 days for pre-production samples, 60–75 days for bulk production, 21–35 days ocean transit. Total order-to-DC is typically 110–140 days for first-run programmes.
