Heritage & Scale

Six Decades of Home Textile Manufacturing from India

Abhi Home has manufactured home textiles in India since 1965. Across three generations, the business has grown from a single-loom operation into a vertically integrated private-label OEM — running fabric development, printing, dyeing, embroidery, making-up and QA under one roof. We supply home textile to international retailers, importers and hospitality groups across Europe, North America, Asia-Pacific and the Middle East.

The business operates entirely through private-label contract manufacturing. We do not sell under our own brand in retail. Every programme runs under the buyer's care label, tech pack, packaging spec and compliance framework — with full chain-of-custody documentation against OEKO-TEX, GOTS and GRS where required.

Manufacturing Scope

An End-to-End Indian Facility

Fabric

Direct cotton sourcing, in-house yarn-dye and piece-dye, certified GOTS organic and GRS recycled supply chains.

Print

Hand block, screen, rotary and digital printing. Reactive, pigment and discharge systems. Low-MOQ digital for development programmes.

Weave

Power-loom dobby and jacquard, waffle, herringbone, terry, and hand-loom constructions. Custom GSM and thread-count programmes.

Hand-Work

Hand embroidery (cord, satin, chain, French knot), kantha hand-quilting, hand-tufting, patchwork, appliqué and crochet.

Making-Up & QA

Stitching, finishing, inspection against AQL 2.5 (major) / 4.0 (minor), in-house metal detection, third-party lab testing pre-shipment.

Compliance

OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GOTS, GRS, BCI, Sedex SMETA, SA 8000, BSCI — certificates issued per shipment.

Why India

Why International Buyers Source Home Textiles From India

India holds three compounding advantages for home-textile manufacturing: the world's second-largest cotton fibre supply, the most extensive hand-craft heritage (block printing, kantha quilting, hand embroidery, crochet, hand-tufting), and the largest OEKO-TEX-certified production base of any sourcing country. No other origin combines all three at export scale.

Beyond the raw supply-chain advantages, India offers something structurally unique: a simultaneous mastery of hand-craft technique and industrial-scale compliance. A certified Indian manufacturer can produce 500 pieces of hand-block-printed cotton on Monday and 5,000 pieces of power-loom jacquard on Tuesday — under the same OEKO-TEX scope, the same SMETA audit, and the same vendor-portal compliance framework. No other sourcing region offers this combination.

Frequently Asked

Buyer Questions

Who are the leading home textile manufacturers in India?

India has hundreds of export-grade home-textile manufacturers running vertically integrated facilities with OEKO-TEX, GOTS, GRS, BCI and Sedex certifications. These mills supply international retailers under private-label OEM programmes across bedding, cushions, bath textiles, kitchen linen, throws and pouffes. Abhi Home has manufactured in India since 1965.

What product categories do Indian home textile manufacturers cover?

The standard export categories are bedding (duvet covers, sheet sets, quilts), bath textiles (towels, bath mats, robes), kitchen linen (tea towels, aprons, table linen), cushions and cushion covers, throws and blankets, and pouffes and floor seating. Abhi Home manufactures across all six categories in-house.

Which certifications matter when sourcing home textiles from India?

OEKO-TEX Standard 100 for product safety, GOTS for organic cotton chain-of-custody, GRS for recycled content traceability, BCI for sustainably grown conventional cotton, and Sedex SMETA / SA 8000 for social compliance. International retailers typically require the mill to hold a subset of these depending on programme.

What is the typical MOQ for Indian home textile manufacturers?

MOQs vary by category and construction. Abhi Home keeps MOQs low across all categories — cushions, bedding, bath, kitchen linen, throws and pouffes — sized to programme requirements. Sampling-stage MOQs are kept lowest to support buyer development cycles.

How long does an Indian home textile manufacturer take to deliver?

Counter-samples 10–14 days, pre-production samples 21 days, bulk production 45–90 days depending on category, plus 21–35 days ocean transit. Total order-to-DC typically runs 95–140 days for first-run programmes.

Can Indian home textile manufacturers supply the US market?

Yes. Major Indian home-textile manufacturers supply US department stores, home-specialty chains and D2C brands under CPSIA, California Prop 65 and FTC fibre-labelling compliance. Abhi Home additionally operates a US-based trading entity for buyers who prefer domestic contracting with direct-from-India shipment.
