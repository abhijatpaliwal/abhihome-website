Spain Trade Intelligence

What Spain Importers Need to Know Specifically

Beyond the generic sourcing process, successful India-to-Spain bedding programmes hinge on four country-specific realities: the India–Spain shipping corridor, the duty and trade-agreement landscape, Spain-specific textile and product compliance, and Spain retailer commit cycles. Each is covered below.

Trade Lane

India-to-Spain Ocean Routing

Sailings from Mundra and Nhava Sheva via the Suez Canal reach Valencia in 20–22 days on Maersk and MSC services — Valencia is the busiest Mediterranean container port and the primary Spanish entry point for Indian home textiles. Barcelona handles Catalonia and southern-France onward cargo. Algeciras is Europe's largest transhipment hub — many containers bound for Portugal, northern Spain and West Africa are transhipped here. Rail connections from Valencia reach Madrid in under 10 hours.

Duty & FTA

Spain Import Duty & Origin

Bedding products classify under HS 6302.21 (printed cotton bed linen), HS 6302.31 (cotton bed linen, other) and HS 6302.22/6302.32 (synthetic bed linen). Spain imports under the EU Common Customs Tariff; the India–EU FTA is under negotiation but not yet in force. MFN duties on HS Chapter 63 home-textile lines apply. Spanish import VAT (21%) is levied on CIF value plus duty. Spain operates one of Europe's most efficient customs clearance regimes through the Agencia Tributaria e-Customs system; we provide the HS breakdown and certificates of origin with every commercial invoice.

Compliance

Spain-Specific Regulation

EU REACH Regulation 1907/2006 applies for SVHC, AZO-dye and restricted substances. EU Textile Labelling Regulation 1007/2011 requires fibre-composition labels in Spanish (and Catalan, Galician or Basque for regional markets where applicable). Royal Decree 928/1987 on textile product labelling and Royal Decree 3484/2000 on consumer-goods safety apply. For contract-hospitality cushions, UNE-EN 1021-1/2 (cigarette and match ignition) is specified. We supply OEKO-TEX, GOTS and Sedex SMETA documentation with every shipment.

Commit Cycle

Spain Buying Calendar

Spanish home-textile buyers commit AW ranges in late January for August on-shelf delivery and SS ranges in September for February delivery. Spain's principal home-decor trade show is Intergift Madrid (February and September). Habitat Valencia (September) is the furnishing-focused alternative drawing regional and hospitality buyers. Many Spanish retailers also attend Maison&Objet Paris and Heimtextil Frankfurt for wider European sourcing. Spain's strong tourism-driven hospitality sector runs separate contract commits timed to refurbishment calendars in Q1 and Q4.

Spain Buyer FAQ

Questions Spain Importers Ask

Which Spain ports does Abhi Home ship bedding programmes to?

We ship from Mundra and Nhava Sheva in India to Valencia, Algeciras, Barcelona in Spain. Typical ocean transit is 18 to 24 days plus customs clearance. For smaller orders we can consolidate via LCL groupage services direct to Spain inland container depots or bonded warehouses.

What is the MOQ for private-label bedding programmes to Spain?

Minimum order quantity depends on construction. We support low MOQs across printed, yarn-dyed and jacquard bedding constructions, sized to your programme requirements. Sampling-stage MOQs are kept low to support buyer development cycles. For first-container pilot runs we accept blended MOQs across a curated range of SKUs.

Are your bedding programmes compliant with Spain retailer requirements?

Yes. We manufacture to OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GOTS, GRS and BCI certifications, and supply full EU REACH-compliant documentation. For Spanish vertical retail groups we support short lead times on repeat programmes where fabric and print separations are pre-approved.

What is the lead time from order confirmation to Spain delivery?

Typical lead time is 70 to 90 days from order confirmation to Spain port: 45 to 60 days for production including sampling approval, plus 18 to 24 days of ocean freight. Expedited timelines are possible for repeat programmes where fabric, print separations and construction are already approved.

Can you handle Spain retailer labelling, tickets and packaging?

Yes. Our in-house finishing team produces swing tags, care labels, barcoded SKU tickets and retailer-specific inner and outer packaging. We regularly ship floor-ready, shelf-ready and dropship-ready bedding programmes for Spain retailers with bespoke compliance artwork in the required language(s).
