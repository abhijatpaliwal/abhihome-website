Italy Trade Intelligence

What Italy Importers Need to Know Specifically

Beyond the generic sourcing process, successful India-to-Italy throws programmes hinge on four country-specific realities: the India–Italy shipping corridor, the duty and trade-agreement landscape, Italy-specific textile and product compliance, and Italy retailer commit cycles. Each is covered below.

Trade Lane

India-to-Italy Ocean Routing

Direct sailings from Mundra and Nhava Sheva via the Suez Canal reach Genoa in 20–22 days — the shortest India-to-EU ocean transit on major lanes. La Spezia handles Tuscan and central-Italy cargo. Trieste is preferred for north-Italy, central European and Balkan onward distribution, with rail connections to Vienna, Munich and Budapest. Livorno handles leather and fashion adjacencies.

Duty & FTA

Italy Import Duty & Origin

Throws and blankets classify under HS 6301.30 (cotton blankets and travelling rugs) and HS 6301.40 (synthetic blankets and travelling rugs). Italy imports under the EU Common Customs Tariff; the India–EU FTA is under negotiation but not yet in force. MFN duties on HS Chapter 63 home-textile lines apply. Italian import VAT (22%) — the highest standard VAT rate in the EU-27 — is levied on CIF value plus duty. Italian customs (Agenzia delle Dogane) strictly enforce classification on blended-fibre cushions; we provide detailed HS and composition breakdowns on every commercial invoice.

Compliance

Italy-Specific Regulation

EU REACH Regulation 1907/2006 governs SVHC and AZO-dye restrictions. EU Textile Labelling Regulation 1007/2011 mandates fibre-composition labels in Italian. Italy's Legislative Decree 194/1999 governs fibre-composition disclosure and country-of-origin marking; Made in Italy claims are closely regulated and cannot be used for Indian-origin product. Upholstery and filled cushions for hospitality and contract channels must meet UNI EN 1021-1/2 (cigarette and match tests). We provide OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GOTS and Sedex SMETA documentation per shipment to support Italian retailer compliance.

Commit Cycle

Italy Buying Calendar

Italian home-textile buyers commit AW ranges in late January for August on-shelf delivery and SS ranges in August–September for February delivery. Italian high-end lifestyle and design-led retailers attend HOMI Milano (January and September), the principal Italian home-lifestyle trade show. Salone del Mobile Milano (April) draws contract-hospitality and high-end residential buyers — a key window for design-led Indian suppliers to present custom capability. Proposte Cernobbio (May) is the Italian fabrics-for-furnishing preview attended by the major Italian furniture houses and hospitality groups.

Italy Buyer FAQ

Questions Italy Importers Ask

Which Italy ports does Abhi Home ship throws and blankets to?

We ship from Mundra and Nhava Sheva in India to Genoa, La Spezia, Naples in Italy. Typical ocean transit is 18 to 24 days plus customs clearance. For smaller orders we can consolidate via LCL groupage services direct to Italy inland container depots or bonded warehouses.

What is the MOQ for private-label throws and blankets to Italy?

Minimum order quantity depends on construction. We support low MOQs across woven, printed and embroidered throw constructions. Sampling-stage MOQs are kept low to support buyer development cycles. For first-container pilot runs we accept blended MOQs across colours and patterns.

Are your throws and blankets compliant with Italy retailer requirements?

Yes. We manufacture to OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GOTS, GRS and BCI certifications, and supply full EU REACH-compliant documentation. We also offer base-textile supply for Italian finishing partners who add decoration or Made-in-Italy finishing steps in-country.

What is the lead time from order confirmation to Italy delivery?

Typical lead time is 70 to 90 days from order confirmation to Italy port: 45 to 60 days for production including sampling approval, plus 18 to 24 days of ocean freight. Expedited timelines are possible for repeat programmes where fabric, print separations and construction are already approved.

Can you handle Italy retailer labelling, tickets and packaging?

Yes. Our in-house finishing team produces swing tags, care labels, barcoded SKU tickets and retailer-specific inner and outer packaging. We regularly ship floor-ready, shelf-ready and dropship-ready throws and blankets for Italy retailers with bespoke compliance artwork in the required language(s).
