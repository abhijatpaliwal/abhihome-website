Certification

“Organic” Only Means Something With a Certificate

A lot of bath mats are sold as organic on the strength of nothing more than a supplier's word. If you are putting an organic claim on a swing ticket in the EU, the UK or the US, you need documentation that survives a compliance review — and increasingly, one that survives a green-claims challenge.

So we work the other way round. We tell you which certificate your programme will actually carry before we quote it, we flag where a specification breaks certification — standard latex non-slip backing is the usual culprit — and we supply the Transaction Certificate with the goods, not just the mill's Scope Certificate. If a fully GOTS programme is not the right commercial fit, we will say so and propose OCS, GRS or BCI instead rather than stretching a claim.

Certification routeGOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard) for full organic programmes; OCS 100 and OCS Blended where organic content is partial; GRS for recycled content; BCI Better Cotton as an entry-level responsible-sourcing route.
What GOTS actually coversGOTS certifies the fibre and the processing chain — spinning, dyeing, tufting, finishing and packing — plus social criteria at each stage. It is why a GOTS bath mat costs more than an “organic cotton” bath mat with no certificate behind it.
Constructions availableEvery construction we make can be produced organic: tufted pile, cut & loop, flat-woven reversible, crochet, shaped and kids' mats.
DyesGOTS-approved low-impact reactive dyes. Undyed and naturally-pigmented cotton available for programmes that want to avoid dyeing altogether.
Standard sizes50×80 cm and 60×90 cm running; custom sizes and formats to brief.
BackingGOTS-compliant backing options, or unbacked. Standard latex non-slip backing is not GOTS-compliant — this is the single most common thing buyers get caught out on, so we flag it at sampling every time.
DocumentationScope Certificate at order stage and a Transaction Certificate with the shipment. We supply both as standard — a mill's scope certificate alone does not prove your specific goods were certified.
MOQ & lead timeLow, flexible MOQs, though certified organic yarn does carry a higher practical minimum than conventional. Sampling 2–3 weeks; certified bulk lead times run slightly longer because of the yarn traceability chain.

Buyer Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a GOTS bath mat and an “organic cotton” bath mat?

GOTS certifies the whole chain — fibre, spinning, dyeing, tufting, finishing and packing — plus social criteria at each stage, and it is independently audited. An uncertified “organic cotton” bath mat is a supplier claim with nothing behind it. If you are making an organic claim to consumers in the EU, UK or US, only the certified route is defensible.

Can a GOTS bath mat have a non-slip backing?

Not with standard latex backing — it breaks GOTS compliance, and this is the single most common thing buyers get caught out on. The options are: supply the mat unbacked, use a GOTS-compliant backing where one fits your specification, or accept OCS certification instead of full GOTS. We raise this at sampling stage every time rather than letting it surface after the order.

Do you supply the certificate with the shipment?

Yes. We supply a Scope Certificate at order stage and a Transaction Certificate with the shipment. Ask any supplier for the Transaction Certificate specifically — a Scope Certificate only proves the mill is certified, not that your particular goods were produced under certification.

Is organic cotton more expensive for bath mats?

Yes, and the gap is mostly in the yarn plus the traceability administration rather than the manufacturing. Certified organic yarn also carries a higher practical minimum quantity than conventional. Where the numbers do not work for a first order, OCS Blended, GRS recycled or BCI Better Cotton give you a credible sourcing story at a lower cost.

GOTS approved, OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GRS certified, OCS 100, BCI member, Sedex registered, SA 8000, BSCI, CTPAT, ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001 and Higg Index verified.
