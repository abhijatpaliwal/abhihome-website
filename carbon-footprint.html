Where Your Trees Grow

Trees for Tribals® — Shahbad, Rajasthan

Our trees are planted in the Shahbad block of Baran District in southeastern Rajasthan — one of India's most ecologically vulnerable regions. The land here belongs to Sahariya tribal communities, whose livelihoods depend on the forest for fuel, fodder, fruit, and medicine.

Through Grow-Trees.com's "Trees for Tribals®" programme, indigenous species like Tectona grandis (teak), Emblica officinalis (amla), Acacia catechu, Dalbergia sissoo (sheesham), and Psidium guajava (guava) are raised in community-run nurseries and planted by local tribal families — who are also paid wages for the work.

Why It Matters

The compounding impact of one tree.

A single mature tree absorbs roughly 22 kg of CO2 per year, releases enough oxygen for two people, cools its surroundings, and prevents soil erosion. Multiply that by the trees on this page.
