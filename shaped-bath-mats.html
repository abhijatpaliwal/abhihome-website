Shaped & Sculpted

Silhouettes That Survive the Wash

A shaped bath mat is the fastest way to make a bath range look designed rather than sourced. It is also the easiest thing to get wrong: cut a silhouette badly, bind the curve badly, and the mat unravels within a season. We tuft with the cut line planned in, die-cut or hand-cut the shape, and bind the full perimeter rather than heat-sealing it — and we tell you at sampling stage when a shape you have designed will not hold up, rather than after your customer has washed it twice. Oval, round, scallop, cloud, arch, heart, star, animal and fully bespoke silhouettes to your own artwork.

Craft

The Shape Is Easy. The Binding Is Not.

Cutting a bath mat into a cloud, an oval or an animal silhouette takes an afternoon. Making that silhouette survive fifty washes takes a bound edge, correctly tensioned around every curve, on a pile that was tufted with the cut line already in mind. That is the part that separates a shaped bath mat you can put your brand on from one that comes back as a return.

So we bind rather than heat-seal, we plan the tufting around the intended silhouette, and we are honest at sampling stage about which shapes will not hold up — thin necks, sharp internal corners and long narrow protrusions are the usual problems. If a shape you have designed will fail, we would rather tell you before the order than after the first customer complaint.

How a shape is madeTufted on a broadloom, then die-cut or hand-cut to the silhouette and bound around the full perimeter. The binding is where a shaped mat lives or dies — a poorly bound curve unravels within a season, so we bind rather than heat-seal.

Buyer Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you produce a bath mat in a completely custom shape?

Yes. Send a flat vector file (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF), or a clean scaled sketch with dimensions, and we will prototype the silhouette. Allow one extra sampling round compared with a rectangular mat, because the die and the edge binding both need approving.

Which shapes are the strongest sellers?

Oval is the highest-volume non-rectangular shape and the easiest to add to an existing range. Scalloped and cloud edges perform well because they read as designed while still merchandising on a standard shelf. Animal and character silhouettes sell hardest in the children's category.

Are there shapes you will not make?

We will make almost anything, but we will not ship something we know will fail. Very thin necks, sharp internal corners and long narrow protrusions are difficult to bind durably and tend to unravel in the wash. When a design has one of those, we will say so and propose the nearest silhouette that will survive laundering.

How are shaped bath mats sized and quoted?

To the bounding box rather than the silhouette, because that is what determines material usage and shelf footprint. Tell us the overall footprint you need and we will work the shape back from it.

Can shaped bath mats be organic or reversible?

Organic, yes — any shape can be produced in GOTS certified cotton. Reversible is more limited, because a die-cut mat needs a bound edge and most reversible constructions are woven flat; oval and scalloped reversible formats work, more complex silhouettes generally do not.
