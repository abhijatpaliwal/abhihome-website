Wholesale kids bath mats: a private label buying guide

By Abhijat Paliwal, founder, Abhi Home®, third-generation Indian home textile manufacturer (since 1965).

Buyers who search for wholesale kids bath mats are usually building a children's bath assortment and finding that many factories treat a child's bath mat as an adult mat cut smaller. A kids' bath mat or bath rug needs a shape that survives the wash, a strict safety brief, the right backing for your certification and paperwork that matches US rules for children's products. Why kids' bath mats are their own category

Shaped cutting, a tighter safety brief and more sampling per design are why many manufacturers avoid this category.

The buyer is a parent and the user is a child, which changes what a good product looks like:

  • The motif has to last. The character or word is why it sells, so it must survive washing.
  • The shape has to hold. A silhouette that loses its outline in the laundry becomes a return.
  • Nothing should come loose. Parents notice trims, eyes and decorations, and so do safety reviewers.
  • Certification carries more weight. Parents look for certified cotton on children's product.

We make kids' bath mats on the same tufting floor, in the same cotton and to the same certifications as our main bath programme. What changes is the size, the cutting and a stricter finishing brief. You can see the current range on our kids' bath mats page.

Constructions that work for children

Most kids' ranges use one or two constructions.

Tufted cotton bath mats are made by punching yarn into a base cloth to form the pile. Tufting is how we build motifs, lettering and faces into a children's mat, because the design is carried by the pile itself rather than printed on top. A tufted mat can be single-sided, with an anti-skid backing underneath, or reversible, with two finished faces and no backing. Every design in our current kids' range is tufted cotton with a non-slip backing. See our tufted cotton bath mats for the wider construction.

Reversible tufted mats have two usable faces and no backing at all, because both sides are used. That makes them easy to flip between washes, but they give up the grip a backing provides. For a children's bathroom where grip is a priority, the single-sided version is usually the better choice. Our reversible cotton bath mats page explains the trade off.

Crochet bath mats are hand made in cotton yarn, with a flatter feel than tufted pile. See our crochet bath mats for the construction.

Shapes, binding and motifs

Shape is what sells a children's bath rug. Stars, clouds, hearts, flowers, rainbows and animal silhouettes are the families buyers ask for most, along with simple rounds and half rounds that carry a motif. A rectangle with a tufted motif is the value entry point into a kids' range.

Shaped bath mats are tufted with the cut line planned in, then die cut or hand cut to the silhouette and bound around the full perimeter. The binding is where a shaped mat succeeds or fails. A curve that is heat sealed rather than bound can unravel within a season, so we bind every shaped kids' mat, and the anti-skid backing is cut to the same silhouette. Our shaped bath mats page covers the method in more detail.

Narrow necks, thin protrusions and sharp internal corners are hard to bind durably. When a design will not survive laundering, we say so at sampling and propose the nearest version that will.

For artwork, a flat vector file is ideal, and a clean scaled sketch with dimensions is enough to prototype from. One point needs care: characters belong to someone. We will not reproduce a third party's licensed character unless you can show that you hold the licence. Original characters and generic shapes avoid the question entirely.

Writing the children's safety brief

Write the safety brief down before sampling. We build every children's mat to these points:

  • No small detachable parts. No beads, buttons or loose trims that a child could pull off.
  • No glued on features. Eyes, noses and decorations are tufted into the pile or stitched, never glued.
  • Secured trims. Where a design has pom poms or appliqué, they are stitched to withstand pulling and laundering.
  • Motif in the pile. Building the design into the pile, rather than printing it on, keeps the motif through the wash cycle a child's mat actually gets.
  • Tested materials. OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 testing for harmful substances is our default on bath programmes.

If your market has an additional children's product requirement, put it in the brief from the first sample.

Anti-skid backing and organic certification

Grip matters in a children's bathroom, so we generally recommend a non-slip backing on single-sided kids' mats. The common options are latex, spray latex, hot melt and TPR rubber, and latex free alternatives are available where a market or a retailer specification requires them. A reversible mat cannot take any backing.

The backing decision interacts with certification, and this is where buyers are most often caught out. GOTS certified organic cotton is our default recommendation for children's bath mats, because organic certification is what parents look for. However, standard latex non-slip backing is not GOTS compliant. If you want both a GOTS claim and a non-slip back, there are three routes to discuss at sampling:

  1. Supply the mat unbacked, or choose a reversible construction with no backing.
  2. Use a GOTS compliant backing where one fits the specification.
  3. Choose a different certification route for that item.

We confirm in writing which certificate a programme will carry before we quote it. Our organic bath mats page explains what the certificate covers, and our GOTS certification and OEKO-TEX certification pages show what we hold. The standard itself is published by GOTS.

US rules for a children's bath rug

If you sell into the United States, the first question is whether your bath mat is a children's product. Under 16 CFR 1200.2, a children's product is a consumer product designed or intended primarily for children 12 years of age or younger. The determination weighs the stated intended use, packaging and promotion, consumer recognition and CPSC age guidelines. A mat sold as part of a kids' range, in a child's shape with child focused packaging, is very likely to fall inside that definition. Confirm the determination for your product with your compliance adviser or test lab.

If it is a children's product, these points follow:

  • Children's Product Certificate. The CPSC guidance on the Children's Product Certificate explains that domestic manufacturers and importers must certify compliance in a written certificate, based on tests by a CPSC accepted third party laboratory. The same page notes that, beginning July 8, 2026, importers of most regulated consumer products must electronically file certificate data with US Customs and Border Protection.
  • Small carpet flammability. Under 16 CFR 1631, a small carpet or rug is one with no dimension greater than 6 ft and an area no greater than 24 sq ft, which covers most bath mats. Small carpets and rugs appear on the CPSC list of rules requiring third party testing for children's products, alongside total lead content.
  • Lead content. Under 16 CFR 1500.91, textiles of natural fibers such as cotton, dyed or undyed, are determined not to exceed the lead limits. That determination excludes textiles carrying treatments or applications that are not entirely dyes, so ask your lab how a backing, a print or a trim on the mat should be tested.
  • Fiber labels. Textile labeling rules under 16 CFR 303.16 require the generic fiber names and percentages, the name or RN of the responsible company, and the country of origin.

We supply specification details and our certificates for your file; the importer or US manufacturer issues the certificate itself.

Sizes, palettes and coordinated sets

Kids' mats are usually smaller than adult bath mats. On our kids' bath mats page, 40 x 60 cm and 45 x 70 cm are the practical running sizes. Shaped mats are quoted to the bounding box rather than the silhouette, so tell us the overall footprint you need on the shelf and we work the shape back from that.

Palette does a lot of work in a kids' range. Tonal single colours suit nursery buyers who want a calm room, while multicolour designs let one SKU carry a full palette. A tufted rainbow arch is the clearest example: each band is tufted so it sits slightly proud of the next, which keeps the colours crisp after washing.

Kids' bath mats sell best as a story. Two colourways of one shape make a simple two SKU shelf, and an animal group grows over seasons. A kids' mat can also sit inside a coordinated bath set from one dye lot. Our bath rug sets page covers set formats, and our guide to wholesale bathroom rug sets explains how sets are specified and packed.

How a wholesale kids' bath mat order runs

A kids' order adds one step for the shape:

  1. Brief. Shapes or artwork, sizes, colours, construction, backing, certification and target price.
  2. Shape prototype. For a new silhouette we prototype the cut and the binding before anything else, because that is where a shaped mat succeeds or fails.
  3. Counter sample. The full mat in your colours, with backing and labels, checked against the safety brief.
  4. Wash test. We wash the sample to check the shape, the binding, the motif and the backing before bulk is approved.
  5. Bulk production. Made and checked against the approved sample.
  6. Documents and shipping. Certificates for your file, then packing to your specification.

We work to flexible MOQs, and small first orders are welcome when a retailer is testing children's bath. Request a quote against your brief and we will confirm timings against your ship window. Our guide to bath mat MOQs, lead times and sampling explains how those conversations usually run.

Questions to ask before you shortlist

Ask any factory:

  • Is the motif built into the pile, or printed on top?
  • Is every shaped mat bound around the full perimeter, and will you tell me if my shape will not hold?
  • Are any features glued on, and how are trims secured?
  • Which backing do you recommend, and does it fit the certification I want to claim?
  • Which certificates apply to this yarn, and will a transaction certificate travel with the shipment where needed?

Get the answers in writing. Our guide on how to choose a wholesale bath mat supplier goes further on vetting.

Frequently asked questions

Are your kids' bath mats made from cotton?

Yes. Our kids' bath mats are 100% cotton as standard, and any design can be produced in GOTS certified organic cotton on request, which we recommend for children's ranges.

Can a kids' bath mat have a non-slip backing and a GOTS claim?

Not with standard latex backing, which is not GOTS compliant. The options are an unbacked or reversible mat, a GOTS compliant backing where one fits the specification, or a different certification route. We raise this at sampling every time.

Can you make a bath mat in our character or brand shape?

Yes. Send a flat vector file or a clean scaled sketch with dimensions and we will prototype the silhouette. We will not reproduce a third party's licensed character without evidence that you hold the licence.

Is a kids' bath mat a children's product in the US?

If it is designed or intended primarily for children 12 or younger, it is treated as a children's product, which brings third party testing and a Children's Product Certificate. Confirm the determination for your product with your compliance adviser or lab.

Does Abhi Home® make kids' bath mats under our brand?

Yes. Abhi Home® works only as an OEM / private label manufacturer, so every mat carries your brand, your labels and your packaging. We work to flexible MOQs; request a quote against your brief.
