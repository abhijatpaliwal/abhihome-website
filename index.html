Since 1965

Six decades of experience, engineered for scale today.

Abhi Home has manufactured home textiles in India since 1965. That long track record now runs on modern, high-capacity production — combining deep manufacturing know-how with the reliability, throughput and quality control that global retail programmes depend on.

Led by Abhijat Paliwal, the business runs on the three things large buyers care about most: consistent quality, dependable supply, and competitive cost.
