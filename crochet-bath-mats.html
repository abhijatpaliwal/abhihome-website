Craft

Hand-Crocheted, Not Simulated

There is a lot of product on the market described as crochet that is a machine-tufted imitation of a crochet loop. Ours is hand-crocheted in cotton yarn by artisans, then blocked and finished. You can tell the difference in the hand, and so can the customer — the slight irregularity between pieces is the product, not a defect, and it is worth saying so on the swing ticket.

TechniqueHand-crocheted in cotton yarn by skilled artisans, then blocked and finished. Not machine-knitted or machine-tufted — the loop structure and the slight irregularity are the point of the product.
ConstructionsRound and oval spiral crochet, granny-square panels, ribbed and textured stitch grounds, chindi and recycled-yarn crochet, and crochet-edged flat-woven grounds where a lighter mat is wanted.
FibresCotton yarn as standard, including recycled cotton and chindi (reclaimed cut cotton). GOTS certified organic cotton yarn available.

Handle and dryingCrochet dries faster than tufted pile because of the open loop structure, and it launders well. It is a lighter, more textural mat — it will not give the plush underfoot feel of a deep tufted pile, and we would not pitch it as if it does.
Sustainability storyThe strongest of any construction we make: hand-produced, low-energy, and available in recycled and reclaimed cotton. It is a genuine artisan-craft story rather than a manufactured one.
MOQ & lead timeLow, flexible MOQs, though hand-crochet is capacity-bound rather than machine-bound, so large volumes need longer lead times booked earlier. Talk to us early on seasonal programmes.

Styles

Crochet Constructions

Each of these can be produced in conventional, recycled or GOTS certified organic cotton yarn, and recoloured to your palette.

Round spiral

Worked outward from the centre in a continuous spiral. The classic crochet bath mat silhouette and the strongest seller.

Oval spiral

The same technique in an oval footprint — more practical in front of a bath or vanity than a true round.

Granny square

Crocheted panels joined into a rectangle. Lets you use several colours in one piece and reads as unmistakably handmade.

Ribbed & textured stitch

Denser stitch grounds with a raised rib. The most absorbent of the crochet constructions.

Chindi & recycled

Crocheted from reclaimed cut cotton. Heavier, more rustic, and the best sustainability story on the page.

Crochet-edged

A flat-woven cotton ground with a hand-crocheted border — the craft cue at a lower price point.

Buyer Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is your crochet bath mat genuinely hand-crocheted?

Yes — hand-crocheted in cotton yarn by artisans, then blocked and finished. A lot of product sold as crochet is machine-tufted imitation of a crochet loop; ours is not. The slight variation between pieces is inherent to the technique, and worth declaring on the swing ticket rather than treating as a fault.

How absorbent is a crochet bath mat compared with a tufted one?

Less absorbent underfoot than a deep tufted pile, but faster drying because the loop structure is open. Our ribbed and textured stitch grounds are the most absorbent of the crochet constructions. If plush underfoot feel is the priority for your programme, a tufted mat is the right product and we will tell you so.

Can crochet bath mats be made in organic or recycled cotton?

Yes to both. GOTS certified organic cotton yarn, GRS recycled cotton, and chindi — reclaimed cut cotton — are all available. Crochet in recycled or reclaimed yarn gives you the strongest sustainability story of any construction we make.

Do crochet bath mats have a non-slip backing?

Normally not, because bonding a backing to hand-crochet loses the reversibility and the quick-drying behaviour that make it worth buying. We recommend a separate non-slip underlay where the market requires one.

What lead times should we plan for on crochet?

Longer than machine-made constructions, because hand-crochet is limited by artisan capacity rather than machine hours. Sampling is quick; it is volume that needs planning. For a seasonal programme, talk to us early and we will book capacity against your ship window.
