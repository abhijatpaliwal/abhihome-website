Argentina Trade Logistics

How Kitchen Linen Move from Panipat to Argentina Shelves

Origin Ports

We despatch out of Mundra and Nhava Sheva (JNPT), India's two largest container ports, with direct and transhipment sailings to Argentina on Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, ONE and Hapag-Lloyd services.

Argentina Entry Ports

Buenos Aires for Capital Federal and Buenos Aires Province DCs, Rosario for Santa Fe and central-Argentine programmes, La Plata for consolidated Mercosur routing, and Bahía Blanca for southern-Argentina distribution.

Transit & Lead Time

40–50 days ocean transit plus customs. Total order-to-DC lead time is typically 95–125 days from confirmation, depending on fabric development and sampling approval.

Compliance Docs

INTI (Instituto Nacional de Tecnología Industrial) textile-labelling conformity, Resolución 404/2020 and IRAM-aligned Spanish-language labelling, SEDEX SMETA audit reports and OEKO-TEX certificates of compliance per shipment.

Argentina Buyer FAQ

Questions Argentina Importers Ask

Which Argentina ports does Abhi Home ship kitchen linens to?

We ship from Mundra and Nhava Sheva in India to Buenos Aires, Rosario, La Plata in Argentina. Typical ocean transit is 40 to 50 days plus customs clearance. For smaller orders we can consolidate via LCL groupage services direct to Argentina inland container depots or bonded warehouses.

What is the MOQ for private-label kitchen linens to Argentina?

Minimum order quantity depends on construction. Printed tea towels typically start at 1,000 pieces per design. Yarn-dyed and jacquard items start at 500 pieces per SKU. For first-container pilot runs we accept blended MOQs across tea towels, aprons and table linen.

Are your kitchen linens compliant with Argentina retailer requirements?

Yes. We manufacture to OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GOTS, GRS and BCI certifications, and work with Argentine import agents to produce INTI / IRAM-aligned Spanish-language textile labelling and manage Argentine import-licensing requirements per consignment.

What is the lead time from order confirmation to Argentina delivery?

Typical lead time is 95 to 125 days from order confirmation to Argentina port: 45 to 60 days for production including sampling approval, plus 40 to 50 days of ocean freight. Expedited timelines are possible for repeat programmes where fabric, print separations and construction are already approved.

Can you handle Argentina retailer labelling, tickets and packaging?

Yes. Our in-house finishing team produces swing tags, care labels, barcoded SKU tickets and retailer-specific inner and outer packaging. We regularly ship floor-ready, shelf-ready and dropship-ready kitchen linens for Argentina retailers with bespoke compliance artwork in the required language(s).
