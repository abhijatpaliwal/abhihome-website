About the Standard

What is GOTS?

The Global Organic Textile Standard (GOTS) is the world's leading standard for textiles made from organic fibres. It certifies not only that the cotton is grown organically, but that strict environmental and social criteria are met at every stage of processing - spinning, weaving, dyeing, finishing and stitching - backed by independent, on-site certification and a documented chain of custody.

What GOTS Covers

Verified at Every Stage

🌿

Organic Fibre

A GOTS 'organic' grade product contains at least 95% certified organic fibre, verified back to the farm.

💧

Environmental Criteria

Restricted chemical inputs, wastewater treatment and lower-impact dyeing and finishing throughout processing.

🤝

Social Criteria

Fair labour standards aligned with ILO conventions across every certified facility.

🔗

Chain of Custody

Every step is documented so organic content can be traced and proven from farm to finished product.

Why It Matters to Buyers

Organic You Can Prove

For retailers and brands, GOTS turns 'organic' from a marketing claim into a verifiable fact. It satisfies retailer compliance teams, supports premium organic positioning at shelf, and gives end customers independent assurance. Because the standard is audited annually by accredited bodies, your sourcing story stands up to scrutiny.

Buyer FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask

Is Abhi Home GOTS certified?

Yes - Abhi Home manufactures GOTS-certified organic cotton home textiles, with scope and transaction certificates available for confirmed orders.

How is GOTS different from OCS?

GOTS certifies organic fibre plus environmental and social criteria across the supply chain; OCS verifies organic fibre content only. We hold both.

Which products can be GOTS certified?

Organic cotton bedding, cushion covers, throws, and kitchen and bath linen - certified ranges and MOQs confirmed per programme.
