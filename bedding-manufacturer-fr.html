France Trade Logistics

How Bedding Programmes Move from India to France Shelves

Origin Ports

We despatch out of Mundra and Nhava Sheva (JNPT), India's two largest container ports, with direct and transhipment sailings to France on Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, ONE and Hapag-Lloyd services.

France Entry Ports

Le Havre for Île-de-France and northern DCs, Marseille-Fos for southern and Rhône-corridor distribution, Dunkirk for cross-channel programmes, and Rotterdam for transhipment into eastern France.

Transit & Lead Time

24–28 days ocean transit plus customs. Total order-to-DC lead time is typically 78–98 days from confirmation, depending on fabric development and sampling approval.

Compliance Docs

EU REACH & CE-compliant test reports, fibre composition labels per EU Textile Regulation 1007/2011, SEDEX SMETA audit reports and GOTS / OEKO-TEX certificates of compliance issued per shipment.

France Buyer FAQ

Questions France Importers Ask

Which France ports does Abhi Home ship bedding programmes to?

We ship from Mundra and Nhava Sheva in India to Le Havre, Marseille-Fos, Dunkirk in France. Typical ocean transit is 24 to 28 days plus customs clearance. For smaller orders we can consolidate via LCL groupage services direct to France inland container depots or bonded warehouses.

What is the MOQ for private-label bedding programmes to France?

Minimum order quantity depends on construction. Printed bedding typically starts at 300 pieces per SKU. Yarn-dyed and jacquard bedding starts at 500–1,000 pieces per SKU depending on loom width. For first-container pilot runs we accept blended MOQs across a curated range of SKUs.

Are your bedding programmes compliant with France retailer requirements?

Yes. We manufacture to OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GOTS, GRS and BCI certifications, and supply EU REACH-compliant documentation. For French buyers we can additionally provide Duty of Vigilance Law due-diligence evidence and SEDEX SMETA audit reports per shipment.

What is the lead time from order confirmation to France delivery?

Typical lead time is 78 to 98 days from order confirmation to France port: 45 to 60 days for production including sampling approval, plus 24 to 28 days of ocean freight. Expedited timelines are possible for repeat programmes where fabric, print separations and construction are already approved.

Can you handle France retailer labelling, tickets and packaging?

Yes. Our in-house finishing team produces swing tags, care labels, barcoded SKU tickets and retailer-specific inner and outer packaging. We regularly ship floor-ready, shelf-ready and dropship-ready bedding programmes for France retailers with bespoke compliance artwork in the required language(s).
